Service 01

🚛 Full Truckload (FTL)

When your shipment fills an entire truck, FTL is the most efficient and cost-effective option. Your load gets a dedicated truck — no stops, no sharing, no delays. Direct pick-up to direct delivery with full GPS tracking throughout.

FTL is ideal for large manufacturers, distributors and shippers moving high volumes of freight on a regular or one-time basis.

  • ✅ Dedicated truck for your load only
  • ✅ Faster transit — no consolidation stops
  • ✅ Reduced handling — lower damage risk
  • ✅ GPS tracked from pick-up to delivery
  • ✅ Flexible scheduling to fit your timeline
  • ✅ Competitive flat-rate pricing

Service 02

📦 Less Than Truckload (LTL)

Don't need a full truck? LTL lets you pay only for the space your freight actually uses. We consolidate smaller shipments from multiple clients into one efficient load — saving you money without sacrificing reliability or speed.

LTL is perfect for small to mid-size businesses shipping pallets or partial loads on a regular basis without the cost of a full truck.

  • ✅ Pay only for the space you use
  • ✅ Cost-effective for smaller shipments
  • ✅ Regular scheduled LTL lanes available
  • ✅ Palletised and non-palletised freight accepted
  • ✅ Full tracking and delivery confirmation
  • ✅ Flexible pick-up and delivery windows
