Our Story

Started With One Truck. Built Into a Trusted Name.

Ingino Logistics was founded by Michael Ingino in Winston Salem, North Carolina with a single truck and a commitment to doing things right. What started as local freight runs across NC quickly grew into a full-service trucking and logistics operation covering the entire country.

Over a decade later, we have delivered hundreds of loads across 48 states — earning a reputation for reliability, professionalism and the kind of personal service that larger carriers simply can't offer. Every load matters to us because we built this business one delivery at a time.

We are proud to call Winston Salem home and proud to serve businesses of all sizes — from small local shippers to large national accounts — with the same level of care and commitment on every single run.
