About Us

Built in Winston Salem. Trusted Across the Nation.

Ingino Logistics was founded by Carlos Alberto Ruiz with a single truck and a commitment to doing things right. Over 10 years later, we have delivered hundreds of loads across 48 states — earning a reputation for reliability, professionalism and personal service that larger carriers simply can't match.

We are proud to be a Winston Salem business and prouder still to be the freight partner that our clients count on — time after time, load after load.
